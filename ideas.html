Key ideas

Five concepts support the project.

Digital responsibility

Using technology and information consciously, and thinking about the consequences of what we read, share, publish and communicate online.

Critical thinking

Asking questions about the evidence, context, purpose and reliability of information before accepting it as true.

Misinformation and disinformation

False or misleading content can move quickly between websites and platforms, which makes reliable and inaccurate claims hard to tell apart.

Source credibility

Deciding whether a source can reasonably be trusted. It should not depend only on how professional a website looks.

Media and Information Literacy

Skills to access, analyze, evaluate and create information and media content critically and responsibly, according to UNESCO.
