About the project

Digital media is part of everyday life. We use websites, social media and videos to communicate, study, work and find information. But the amount of information online also creates a challenge, so it is important to question information, evaluate sources and make responsible decisions.

The project is also connected to English language learning. Reading, comparing and discussing information from different sources gives students a real reason to use English while developing critical awareness.

Problem question

How can we foster critical thinking about the information circulating on the Internet to promote responsible use of digital media?

General objective

To foster critical thinking in the digital environment through the analysis and evaluation of information circulating on the Internet, in order to promote a responsible and conscious use of digital media.

Specific objectives

  • Foster critical thinking in the digital environment.
  • Analyze and evaluate information circulating on the Internet.
  • Promote responsible and conscious use of digital media.
